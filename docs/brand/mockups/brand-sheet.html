The family

Both marks sit on the same 32-unit pixel grid with crisp edges, bone on void, one green. Green Sky is a planet; Greenfield is a field inside a selection box: the blank canvas, already being worked.

Sizes

Scale in whole multiples of the grid so the pixels stay square. Below 64 px, use the small mark, redrawn on a 16-unit grid: 16 px for the favicon and top bar, 32 px beside text.

Wordmark and marketing Cormorant Garamond 300 and 400, italic for emphasis. Shared with greensky.tech.
App chrome IBM Plex Sans 12 px controls, 13 px chat, 600 for section heads. The working face of the editor.
Labels and values X 128 · W 326 Tabular numbers in every field, so values don't jump while you drag. Labels are 11 px, uppercase, tracked.
01 · Square

No rounded corners in the chrome. Radius belongs to the user's designs, not to ours.

02 · Rules, not cards

Panels are split by hairlines. Nothing floats on a shadow except menus and the comment composer.

03 · Green is you

Your selection, focus and primary buttons are field green. Nothing else in the chrome is green.

04 · Ember is the agent

Whatever Claude is touching is outlined in ember, so you can always tell your edits from its edits.
